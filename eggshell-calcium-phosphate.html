PROJECT DETAILS

Eggshell-Derived Calcium Phosphate Powder

This final-year project investigates the effect of calcination dwell time on synthesized eggshell-derived calcium phosphate powder. The heating ramp rate is fixed at 10 °C/min and the calcination temperature at 800 °C, with dwell times of 2, 4 and 6 hours.
